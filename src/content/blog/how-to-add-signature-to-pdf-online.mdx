## Types of PDF Signatures

Before you sign, it helps to understand the difference between signature types:

### Electronic Signature (e-Signature)
A digital image of your handwritten signature placed on the PDF. This is what most people need for everyday documents like contracts, forms, and letters. It's legally valid in most countries for standard agreements.

### Digital Signature (Cryptographic)
A cryptographically secured signature that proves document integrity and signer identity. Required for regulated industries (banking, government, legal). Needs a certificate from a trusted authority.

## Is an Electronic Signature Legally Valid?

In most countries, yes — for standard business and personal documents:

- **Malaysia** — E-signatures are recognised under the Digital Signature Act 1997 and Electronic Commerce Act 2006
- **USA** — Valid under ESIGN Act and UETA
- **EU** — Recognised under eIDAS regulation
- **UK** — Valid under Electronic Communications Act 2000

Always check with a legal professional for documents that require certified or notarised signatures.

## Common Questions

**Can the other party verify my signature?**  
An electronic signature (image) can't be cryptographically verified. For documents requiring proof of signing, use a dedicated e-signature platform that issues audit trails.

**Can I reuse my saved signature?**  
Currently, each session starts fresh. Save your signature image and re-upload it if needed.

**Does signing change the PDF content?**  
The signature is embedded as a visual element. Text, form fields, and other content remain intact.
